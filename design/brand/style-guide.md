## 1. The brand in one sentence

Metis helps leaders see where the company is investing and why, while project teams run their daily work on the same source of data, under control. The identity says the same thing: a weighty serif says "trustworthy, governed"; the letter i in the accent colour is "intelligence"; the dot of the i is a diamond, the familiar symbol of milestones and approval steps on the WBS and the roadmap, and it says "governance". In Greek mythology Metis is the goddess of wisdom and counsel, which suits a decision tool. The palette stays small: one ink, one accent, one paper.

## 2. Logo

### 2.1 Construction

The wordmark "Metis" is built from the glyph outlines of Fraunces 700 (optical size 144) and converted to vector paths, so it does not depend on the fonts installed on a machine.

| Detail | Geometry, in the coordinate system of the wordmark file | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| The letter i | The stem of the i (without its original dot) is filled with the accent colour, the other letters with ink | Intelligence is at the centre of the name |
| Diamond | A square rotated 45 degrees, half-diagonal 10.5, centred exactly on the centre of the original dot of the i | Milestone, approval step: governance |
| Mark | The M of Fraunces, with a diamond in the same accent colour above the centre of the M, 7 units from its top | A standalone mark that repeats the diamond of the wordmark |

The glyphs are spaced 1.5 units apart. Do not stretch, slant or change the typeface. Putting the diamond in the notch of the M was tried, but the notch of Fraunces is too narrow and the diamond covers the thin stroke, so it sits above the M.

### 2.3 Rules of use

- The minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of the letter e of the wordmark; put no text, rule or image inside that area.
- Minimum size: the wordmark 96px wide on screen, 24mm in print; the mark 24px, and under 32px use the small version.
- On a photo or a multicoloured background use the one-colour white version, or place the logo on an ink tile.
- Only three colours appear in the logo: ink, violet and white; no gradient, no shadow, no outline.
- The diamond always goes with the letter i or the letter M and is never split off as a pattern. In the interface, a small ink diamond remains the milestone symbol of the WBS; the violet diamond belongs to the logo only.
- Do not retype "Metis" in a live font where the logo is needed; the app header uses the SVG file.

### 2.4 Rebuilding

`tools/` holds the whole generator: `npm install`, `./fetch.sh` downloads Fraunces, `node extract.js` takes the outlines of the word "Metis" (without the dot of the i), `node gen-logo.js ../logo` writes the set of files. Do not edit the exported SVG files by hand.

### 3.3 Status colours

Each status has three values: the dot or fill colour (`--good`), the text colour that reaches 4.5:1 on the pale background (`--good-text`) and the pale background (`--good-bg`). The text colours are darker than the dot colours because the dot colours reach only about 3.2 (green) and 3.1 (amber) on their pale backgrounds. An orange level, "serious", is added for the four-level scale Low, Medium, High, Critical.

| Role | Dot | Text | Background | Used in Metis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| good | `#1A9A55` | `#187B4A` | `#E4F5EB` | RAG Green, Done, Pass, Approved |
| waiting | `#C27A0E` | `#946115` | `#FBF0D9` | RAG Amber, Waiting, Medium, Deferred, Pass with conditions |
| serious | `#C2531A` | `#B04D1B` | `#FDEBDD` | High (priority, risk), Rework |
| critical | `#D6402C` | `#BA3A2B` | `#FCE6E2` | RAG Red, Blocked, Critical, Rejected, Overdue |
| running | `#2563EB` | `#2460E3` | `#E6EEFD` | In Progress, Approved pending application |
| review | `#6D3BEF` | `#6D3BEF` | `#F0EAFE` | Under review, awaiting decision |
| neutral | `#6C6C78` | `#6A6A76` | `#EEEEF1` | Not Started, Low, Draft, Hold, Withdrawn, Closed |

Rule: a status always has colour, shape and text together, never colour alone. RAG uses its own shapes: ● On track (good), ▲ At risk (waiting), ■ Off track (critical).

### 3.4 Chart colours

Following the method of the dataviz skill: a colour does exactly one job, and it is checked by a script, not by eye.

| Job | Rule | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Categorical (series) | Assigned in a fixed order, never cycled; beyond 8 series, group them as "Other" or split the chart | 1 `#6D3BEF`, 2 `#EB6834`, 3 `#1BAF7A`, 4 `#EDA100`, 5 `#E87BA4`, 6 `#008300`, 7 `#2A78D6`, 8 `#E34948` |
| Magnitude (sequential) | One colour, light to dark | A blue scale `#CDE2FB` → `#0D366B` (steps 100 to 700) |
| Two-sided (diverging) | Two poles, warm and cool, with grey in the middle | `#E34948` ↔ `#F0EFEC` ↔ `#2A78D6` |
| Status | Use the colours of section 3.3, with a symbol and a label | |

Check result on a `#FFFFFF` background: the eight colours in the order above pass every check for adjacent pairs (delta E 9.1 at worst for colour-blind vision, 19.6 for normal vision); the first three also pass the check for every pair (use them for scatter plots and maps). Colours 3, 4 and 5 are below 3:1 against the background, so a chart that uses them must have direct labels or an accompanying table of numbers.

### 3.5 Tenant branding

A tenant may change its logo, one accent colour, the sender name of emails and the document templates (BRD TEN-12, PLT-17). So that the interface does not break, a tenant's colour goes only into safe slots:

| Slot | What a tenant can change | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| Tenant logo | The left corner of the top bar, next to the small Metis logo; the PDF cover | The Metis logo on the sign-in page and in the PDF footer |
| Tenant accent colour | The top bar or the colour band of the PDF cover, the main button in emails | Links, the selected item, focus and emphasis buttons in the app keep using violet |
| Type | No | Inter, Newsreader, Manrope |
| Status colours, chart colours | No | Sections 3.3 and 3.4, so that the same status has the same colour in every tenant |

Automatic check when a tenant saves a colour: white or ink text on that colour must reach 4.5:1, and the colour must not be too close to a status colour (delta E under 10 against good, waiting, serious, critical). If it fails, the system rejects it and suggests the nearest darker or lighter step that passes.

## 4. Type

| Role | Typeface | Size and weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting, page title | Newsreader | 30px, 500, letter-spacing -1% | Only for titles |
| Section title | Inter | 18px, 600 | |
| Interface text | Inter | 14px, 400, 500, 600 | Rows, cards, forms |
| Dense table | Inter | 13px, tabular figures | WBS, registry, CR |
| Secondary text | Inter | 12.5px | Meta, captions |
| Section label | Inter | 11px, 600, uppercase, letter-spacing 8% | "NEEDS ATTENTION", "THIS WEEK"; only for section labels, see the type size floor below |
| Menu and buttons | Manrope | 14px, 500 for menus, 600 for buttons | Only these two places use Manrope |
| Large figures (KPI tile) | Inter | 28px, 600, regular figures | Not Newsreader: Metis is a data-dense product, so large figures use the same sans typeface as the rest, for fast and consistent reading |
| Codes and numbers in tables | Inter, tabular figures | The same size as the text around it | `1.1.2`, `CR-001`, `2.4M`, `47d` |
| Logo | Fraunces 700 | Only as outlines in the logo files | Do not load Fraunces into the app |

Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Newsreader, Inter, Manrope.

Type size floor: clickable text (buttons, links, menus, input fields) and content text at least 13px; secondary text that carries meaning (meta, captions, the as-of time of data) at least 12px; 11px only for uppercase section labels and text that repeats information already shown elsewhere. Do not use 11px for actions, numeric values or error messages. When the user zooms to 200%, the layout must not hide or clip text.

## 5. Layout and spacing

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Corner radius | 6px for cards, buttons and input fields; 8px for large panels and modals |
| Borders | 1px `line`; no shadows, except modals and floating menus (one single soft shadow) |
| Sidebar | 232px, rail background, 1px right border; holds the tree Workspace, Portfolio, Program, Project |
| Top bar | 52px: search, tenant and workspace chooser, notifications, user |
| List row | At least 44px high, 16px horizontal padding |
| Table row | 36px high in comfortable mode, 32px in compact mode (section 5.1); 10px horizontal padding; the code and name columns of the WBS stay fixed when scrolling sideways |
| Spacing | 14px between blocks; 20px between large sections; 24px page margin |
| Reading text block | At most about 65 characters per line (charter, reports) |

Default layout: a left sidebar, a top bar and a content area. A project screen adds a project header and a tab bar with Overview, Charter, WBS, Plan, Governance, Focus. The tenant and provider administration screens use their own admin sidebar.

### 5.1 Density and narrow screens

| Mode | List row | Table row | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comfortable (default) | 44px | 36px | Every screen, new users; tokens `--row-list`, `--row-table` |
| Compact | 36px | 32px | WBS, registry, change register when the user chooses it; saved per person (BRD PLT-18) |

|---|---|

## 7. Charts

- Choose the form first, the colour after. A single important number uses a KPI tile, not a chart.
- Bars at most 24px wide, 4px rounded tops, a 2px gap between stacked parts; lines 2px; points at least 8px.
- Grid and axes are thin solid lines in `--grid`, not dashed; no chart with two vertical axes.
- From two series on, always show a legend; direct labels only for important points.
- Text inside a chart uses the text colour, not the series colour.
- Every chart has a matching table of numbers (view as a table) and a tooltip on mouse hover.

## 7a. Accessibility contract for interactive components

The proposed target is WCAG 2.2 level AA (BRD section 17, to be confirmed in D-26). Every component below must allow every task to be done by keyboard; drag and drop and canvases always have an equivalent (BRD BR-41).

| Component | Role for screen readers | Keyboard | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editable table (WBS, registry) | treegrid for the WBS, grid for a flat table | Arrow keys move between cells; Enter or F2 edits; Esc cancels; Space selects a row; the left and right arrows open and close branches | The code and name columns are fixed; the column header is read when the cell changes |
| Navigation tree | tree | Up and down arrows to move, left and right to open and close, type a letter to jump | An open node is read as "expanded" |
| Board | Each column is a list with a name and a count | Tab to a card; the menu "Move to…" (Shift+M) changes the column | Every time the column changes, the new state is read through a live region |
| Canvas (Builder, Tree Diagram) | Has an equivalent list version (list editor, BRD BLD-12) | Tab goes through the nodes in lane order; arrow keys move a node; C to connect, Delete to delete; ? opens the shortcut sheet | No operation can be done by mouse alone |
| Dialog, drawer | dialog with a title | Focus goes to the first element and is kept inside the box; Esc closes; after closing, focus returns to the button that opened it | No dialog opens on top of another dialog |
| Status announcement | A polite live region for Saved; an urgent one for errors and conflicts | Not needed | Not signalled by colour or symbol alone |

The table above is the shared contract. Each interaction pattern also has its own **acceptance matrix**, because the shared contract is not detailed enough to write tests from: it does not say what a `treegrid` must announce when a branch opens, or what a board must read after a column change. The matrix lives in the design system built during Discovery, each row is a testable behaviour, and each pattern has an automated test plus a pass with a real screen reader before the pilot.

| Pattern | The acceptance matrix must state |
|---|---|
| Data table and treegrid (WBS, registry) | The roles and relations of cells, rows and headers; `aria-expanded` and the level of each row; the reading order when columns are fixed; sorting and filtering announced through the live region with the result; the cell being edited and how to cancel; the number of rows after filtering |
| Navigation tree | The roles `tree` and `treeitem`, the level, the open or closed state, the selected row; type to jump; the behaviour when a branch is empty |
| Board with drag and drop | The name and count of each column; how to move a card by menu or keys and where the focus goes after the move; the sentence read after the move (source column, target column, position); how to cancel in the middle of the action |
| Canvas (lifecycle designer, process framework editor) | Each row of the canvas-to-list equivalence matrix (BRD BLD-13): the operation, the mouse gesture, the key, the action in the list, the command sent to the server; the readable name of nodes, lanes and connections (including type and lag); routing points are geometry, but if one needs editing there must be a way in the list |
| Dialog and drawer | The checklist right below |
| Live region | The polite level for completed work, the urgent level for errors and conflicts; no duplicate announcements; not colour or symbol alone |

Checks for every dialog and drawer, written as annotations on the wireframe and as automated component tests: focus goes to the first element on opening; Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside the box; Esc closes; after closing, focus returns to the button or row that opened it; the title is read on opening; an error in the box is read through the urgent live region. A static wireframe shows intent only; the real behaviour is checked with a keyboard and a screen reader before the pilot.

## 8. Language and voice

- The interface is in English by default and Vietnamese is the second language; strings are separated from the code from the start.
- Use the names users recognise: "Change request", "Gate review", "Weekly status", "Input Required".
- Short, active sentences; a button says exactly what happens: "Raise change request", "Publish weekly status", "Approve gate", "Create token".
- Figures always follow the BRD definitions in section 12; the same metric must never show two values on two screens.
