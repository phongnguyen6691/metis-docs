# M10 Project Overview and Focus

Status in the app: partly built. Of its three views only the project overview exists, in the development build on mock data. This page describes the module as the BRD defines it, then what the development build shows today.

## What it is for

M10 gives two read-only views that summarise the WBS, the Overview for a project and Focus for a person, and one queue, My approvals, for the decisions that wait for a person ([BRD v4.5, M10](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#m10-project-overview--focus-ovw)). The two views add no data of their own, since both are derived from the WBS (BR-22), and portfolio and program totals belong to [M15](M15-executive-cockpit-and-workspace-dashboard.md).

The Overview (OVW-01 to OVW-05) shows eight tiles (work items, deliverables, work packages, milestones, gates, blocked, delayed and high risk), charts of overall progress, the status breakdown and progress by category, and lists of what needs attention: the workload by person in charge, upcoming milestones and gates, overdue items, and high and critical risk items. Every number follows the metric definitions of [BRD section 12](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#121-project-metric-definitions) (BR-32), never counts a Category node (BR-24), and states its as-of time and metric version (BR-39). The tile values must equal the Weekly Status summary for the same date. Every tile, chart segment and list entry opens the WBS filtered on what it counts, or the item itself (OVW-04). The project's health is a computed red, amber or green level over delivery, risk, resource and finance, shown beside the status that the project manager declares. A declared Green with a computed Red is flagged as green-but-risky until an override with a rationale exists (OVW-05, BR-08).

Focus (OVW-06 to OVW-08) is the daily page of a person. "Today & This Week" shows the date and a one-line summary, counters for overdue items, items due today, items due in the next seven days and items in progress, and columns for items overdue, due this week and starting this week, each empty column saying why it is empty. "Only mine" narrows the page to the signed-in user. The user can change an item's status or mark it done without opening it, and the change is logged in the item's activity. Focus can also span all the projects of the workspace that the user belongs to.

My approvals (OVW-09) is one queue of everything that waits for the user's decision across the workspaces the user belongs to: change request decisions, gate decisions and votes, charter sign-offs, waiver requests, deliverable approvals and decisions prepared through MCP. A row shows the type, the project, the requester, the age and the due date, and it opens the screen where the decision is taken. The navigation shows how many items the user can act on now, and an item that is blocked by another decision shows its blocker.

In the BRD, project managers and owners use the Overview and Focus, team members use Focus ([section 6](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#6-stakeholders--personas)), and approvers start their daily round in My approvals ([section 7.9](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#79-key-user-journeys)). All nine requirements are P0, and [BRD section 18.2](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#182-release-1-delivery-structure-d-01) plans the module in milestone 1A, including OVW-07 to OVW-09. The decision register assumes responsive web for Focus, Board, Overview and approvals with no native app in the MVP (D-19), and section 12 as the single definition set for metrics (D-24). The register assumes each recommendation until the decision owner confirms or changes it ([BRD section 19.1](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#191-decision-register)), so these are assumptions and not settled decisions.

## What users can do today

One of this module's three views exists. The app is not deployed and has no back end. Its development build runs on mock data and holds one screen of the BRD, screen 24, the project overview, which is this module's Overview; Focus and My approvals are not built. The screen contract of screen 24 names OVW-01 to OVW-05, BR-24, BR-32, BR-39 and D-19. People on the team run the build with `pnpm web`, which opens it in a browser, and the same code builds for iOS and Android. A mock server answers the API calls, so every figure on the screen is a prepared value and not a result computed from a WBS, and no WBS exists yet. The mock data reproduces the wireframe of screen 24: for example 12 work items, 3 of them done, and 25 percent progress.

The screen shows:

- **The project header.** The project name, client, project manager, stage, declared health, framework with its version, and the baseline version and date, or "No baseline". Share is text tagged "Not built yet". Raise change request is a link. Six tabs (Overview, Charter, WBS, Plan, Governance and Focus) and an "All changes saved" line close the header.
- **The heading row.** The title, a freshness line with the data time and the metric version, marked Stale, Partial or Unknown when the data is, a sentence that counts the work items and says that Category rows are excluded, a This project and My items switch, and Export, text tagged "Not built yet".
- **Eight tiles**, in the order of the BRD: Work items, Deliverables, Work packages, Milestones, Approval gates, Blocked, Delayed vs baseline and High risk. Blocked, Delayed and High risk stand out when above zero (OVW-01). Delayed shows "Not baselined" for a project with no baseline.
- **Overall progress.** A whole-number percentage, a bar, and "N of M work items done".
- **The status breakdown.** A stacked bar with a legend. A Table view button turns it into a table of the same figures.
- **Health.** The level declared by the project manager beside the computed level, and a warning strip with the reasons when the declared level is better than the computed one.
- **Progress by category and workload by person in charge**, as bars. The workload bar is scaled to the person with the most open items.
- **Three lists.** Upcoming milestones and gates, overdue items with their days overdue, and high and critical risk items. Each list shows its first rows and a "+ N more" line when the server counts more. An empty upcoming list says so and, when milestones or gates are overdue, how many.

Most controls lead to screens that are not built. Each such link opens the shared page for a screen that is not built, which shows, in the English interface, the title of the link, the sentence "Not built in the first batch.", the screen number, the path, and the filters that came in the address, or "None"; the rule is in the record [Final routes, drill filters in the URL, and unbuilt destinations](../../architecture/decisions/routes-and-unbuilt-destinations.md). On screen 24 these links are:

- every tile, the segments of the status bar (its legend lines where touch targets apply), the category rows, the person rows, the View in WBS button and the + N more lines of the upcoming and risk lists. They open the WBS (screen 26, see [M07](M07-wbs-master.md)) with the server's filter;
- the rows of the three lists, which open a WBS item (screen 28);
- the + N more line of the overdue list and the Focus tab, which open Focus (screen 34);
- the Charter, WBS, Plan and Governance tabs of the project header, which open screens 25, 26 (with no filter), 30 and 12;
- Raise change request, which opens screen 32.

The This project and My items switch and the Table view button work on the screen itself. The switch writes `scope=mine` into the address; in the development build My items shows the same data as This project, because the mock server answers both the same way. The button changes how the status figures are drawn and does not change the address.

| Screen | Where the app links to it | Path | Phase | What opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24, Project overview | The Overview tab, each project in the sidebar tree, and the app root, which redirects to the demo project | `/w/[workspaceId]/projects/[projectId]/overview` | MVP | The built screen |
| 34, Focus: today and this week | The Focus tab of the project header and the + N more line of the overdue list | `/w/[workspaceId]/projects/[projectId]/focus` | MVP | The not-built page |
| 34, Focus: today and this week | My focus in the sidebar | `/me/focus` | MVP | The not-built page |
| 38, My approvals | Approvals in the sidebar | `/me/approvals` | MVP | The not-built page |

Focus (OVW-06 to OVW-08) and My approvals (OVW-09) are not built: nobody can see a Today & This Week page, change a status from it or decide a request from a queue. The counts beside My focus and Approvals in the sidebar come from the mock data of the shell.

When the screen cannot show data it draws a named state instead. Loading shows a sentence and a spinner. No access explains which role is needed, offers Request access as text tagged "Not built yet", and links back to My focus. Project not found links back to My focus too. A server, network or time-out failure offers Try again. An expired session opens a dialog with Sign in again, also text tagged "Not built yet". An answer that the contract rejects names the fields that are wrong. A read-only state is specified for the screen, but the contract has no field for it, so it is not drawn. The demo has three projects in the Creative Studio workspace: one with full data, one with no baseline and stale data, and one that answers "no access". On web, `?mock=` followed by loading, sessionExpired, serverError, invalidData, partial, unknown or longLists in the page address shows the other states.

The layout follows the window width: the full layout with the sidebar from 1280 px, an icon rail from 768 to 1279 px, and one column with a menu drawer below 768 px. Touch targets are at least 44 points below 768 px on web and at every width on the iOS and Android apps. The language select of the top bar switches the screen between English and Vietnamese, and English is the default.

## Where the app differs from the BRD

- **Drill-down ends on a page that says the screen is not built.** OVW-04 says that a tile, a chart segment or a list entry opens the filtered WBS or the item. Only this screen is built, so the same click opens the not-built page, which prints the filter it received. Records: [Final routes, drill filters in the URL, and unbuilt destinations](../../architecture/decisions/routes-and-unbuilt-destinations.md) and [Prove one screen end to end before building more](../../architecture/decisions/acceptance-screen-first.md).
- **The screen computes no metric and builds no filter.** Every number is a snapshot from the server with its as-of time, definition version, freshness and drill filter (BR-32, BR-39). The rule that Category nodes are not counted (BR-24) therefore belongs to the back end, not to the screen. In the development build the snapshots are mock values, which hold no metric rules. Records: [The app never computes a metric or a drill filter](../../architecture/decisions/metrics-are-server-snapshots.md) and [Screens call the real API; a mock server answers in mock builds only](../../architecture/decisions/mock-server-behind-real-api.md).
- **Derived health covers delivery and risk only.** OVW-05 names four dimensions. The contract carries an overall computed level and the reasons for delivery and risk, and no resource or finance level, because the BRD has no rule for those two yet. Record: [Derived health covers delivery and risk only](../../architecture/decisions/derived-health-scope.md).
- **Percentages round down.** Section 12.1 defines overall progress as done work items divided by total work items, times 100, and does not say how to round. The app rounds down to a whole number, so 99.5 percent shows 99 percent and only exactly 100 shows 100 percent (FE-20, decided by the owner on 6 Oct 2026). Record: [Workspace time zone, Intl output, percentages floored](../../architecture/decisions/time-and-number-formatting.md).
- **The drill names `delayed=true` and `scope=mine` are provisional.** WBS-05 has no filter for items late against the baseline, so the Delayed tile opens the WBS with `delayed=true`, and the My items switch writes `scope=mine`. Both are proposals of the front end that the back end will fix, and the record is a draft: [`delayed=true` and `scope=mine` are provisional names](../../architecture/decisions/provisional-drill-parameters.md).
- **The overview also builds as an iOS and an Android app.** D-19 assumes responsive web with no native app in the MVP. The owner chose one code base for web, iOS and Android on 5 Oct 2026 (FE-09), and the screen has three width tiers and 44 point touch targets. Records: [React Native with Expo for web, iOS and Android](../../architecture/decisions/react-native-expo-single-codebase.md) and [Three width tiers and 44 point touch targets](../../architecture/decisions/width-tiers-and-touch-targets.md).
- **Six visual details follow the wireframe, not the brand style guide.** This differs from the style guide and not from the BRD text; it is listed here because the owner has not ruled on it yet. The logo is 24 high (68.58 wide), below the minimum of 96 px of the style guide; the project title is 26 where the display size is 30; the faded button is drawn in muted by the wireframes, by the app (`ghost: 'muted'` in `src/design-system/Button.tsx`), by the table of section 6 of the [style guide](../../design/brand/style-guide.md) and by [style-guide.html](../../design/brand/style-guide.html), although the version history of the original guide says that version 0.5 changed it to the primary text colour (`brand/style-guide.md`, kept in the owner's private store, line 267 of the original); some font sizes sit below the floor of the style guide (freshness and labels 11.5, counts and the Planned label 11, group labels 10.5); a list row has 14 px of horizontal padding where the style guide says 16; and weight 700 is requested in four places and drawn as 600. Record: [Where the wireframe and the style guide disagree, build the wireframe and ask](../../architecture/decisions/wireframe-over-style-guide.md).

## Open questions

- Where do Share and Export lead? Export leans toward PLT-14 and screen 44, and no requirement names Share. Until the owner decides, both show as text tagged "Not built yet" and not as the buttons the wireframe draws. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), lines 694 and 708 of the original; [Final routes, drill filters in the URL, and unbuilt destinations](../../architecture/decisions/routes-and-unbuilt-destinations.md).
- Do the help button and the glossary of PLT-20 get a screen of their own? Until then Help is tagged "Planned V2", and the top bar shows it at desktop width only. Source: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 695 of the original.
- How is project health computed for the resource and finance dimensions, and does each dimension get its own level? OVW-05 names four dimensions and section 12 has no rule for the resource and finance ones. The answer is due before the back end computes project health. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), lines 183 and 702 of the original; [Front-end foundation plan (plan A)](../../history/plans/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation.md), lines 146 to 151 of the original.
- Where does the read-only state come from: which field of the API, and which reason code? PLT-04 lists an archived workspace, a suspended tenant, a frozen revision and a locked baseline field as reasons. The state is specified for the screen and is not drawn. Source: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), lines 293 to 297 and 703 of the original.
- Where do Request access and Sign in again lead, and is the wording of the states right? Both are text tagged "Not built yet", and the sentences are drafts until the owner reviews them. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 707 of the original; [Every response is validated, and every failure is a named state](../../architecture/decisions/validated-contracts-and-error-states.md).
- Does My items filter by the person in charge, by the assignee, or by both? Until the back end answers, the mock server returns the same data as This project. The answer is due before the back end builds the overview API. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 710 of the original; [Overview screen plan (plan B)](../../history/plans/2026-10-06-frontend-overview-screen.md), line 19416 of the original.
- Is this the right rule for the As of line? It names the earliest data time and the worst freshness of the nine metric snapshots, and every definition version in use. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 711 of the original; [Overview screen plan (plan B)](../../history/plans/2026-10-06-frontend-overview-screen.md), line 19418 of the original.
- Should the Blocked tile stand out when it is above zero? OVW-01 says Blocked, Delayed and High risk do, and the spec's description of the screen lists only Delayed and High risk as standing out. The app follows the BRD. Source: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), lines 225 and 714 of the original.
- Do the + N more lines lead to the right pages? The upcoming and risk lists open the WBS with a filter, and the overdue list opens Focus. Source: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 719 of the original.
- Should the upcoming list also filter by date and status? WBS-05 has no filter by date, section 12 defines upcoming milestones and gates as items that are not Done with a target date of today or later, and the overview's upcoming list carries the server's filter, which the mock data sets to the labels milestone and approval gate only. This is a question for the back end. Sources: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), lines 767 to 770 of the original; [Trace and gaps plan](../../history/plans/2026-10-09-frontend-trace-and-gaps.md), lines 9 to 13 of the original; [BRD part 03](../requirements/source/parts/03-modules-c-rules.md), line 128.
- Must any metric be weighted by effort instead of counting work items? The first review asked the client whether it accepts the section 12 formulas for progress, overdue, delayed and workload, which D-24 proposes as the single definition set. Sources: [Pre-feasibility review of the BRD, round 1](../../history/reviews/metis-review-tien-kha-thi.md), line 877 of the original; [BRD part 04](../requirements/source/parts/04-roadmap-decisions.md), line 165.
- How should a frequent approver triage My approvals? The second review asked for next and previous keys and for bulk triage of non-decision information only, never bulk approval. The third review asked for sorting by age or materiality, saved filters by type or materiality and next and previous shortcuts. The disposition of the third review applied sorting, saved views and the keys J and K to the wireframe of screen 38, and it records no answer on bulk approval. OVW-09 names none of these. Sources: [Round 2 review](../../history/reviews/metis-review-vong-2.md), line 419 of the original; [Round 3 review](../../history/reviews/metis-review-vong-3.md), lines 958 and 974 of the original; [Disposition of the round 3 review](../../history/reviews/review-disposition-round3.md), line 83 of the original.
- Can gate approvers delegate their vote or abstain, and how does that count toward the quorum? The BRD lists My approvals (OVW-09) among the things the answer affects. Source: question Q-10 in [BRD part 04](../requirements/source/parts/04-roadmap-decisions.md), line 188.
- Is the workload bar scaled to the person with the most open items, as the app does, or fixed at 16 percent per open item, as the wireframe draws it, which overflows beyond six items? Source: [Front-end foundation spec](../../history/specs/2026-10-05-frontend-foundation-spec.md), line 706 of the original.
