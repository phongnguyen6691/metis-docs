# M15 Executive Portfolio Cockpit and Workspace Dashboard

Status in the app: not built yet. This page describes the module as the BRD defines it, then what the development build shows today.

## What it is for

The cockpit is the top of the product: the CEO-first control tower of the BRD, with the workspace dashboard beside it ([module M15 of BRD v4.5](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#m15-executive-portfolio-cockpit--workspace-dashboard-exe)). It follows two product principles, executive views that surface decisions and exceptions before detail, and exception-based management ([section 4.4](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#44-product-principles)). It helps answer what the company invests in, why and how much, and which projects to fund, accelerate, delay or stop ([section 4.3](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#43-core-management-questions)). In the MVP the cockpit works within one workspace at a time; the cross-workspace executive view follows in V2.

The cockpit opens with a KPI summary of total projects, portfolio investment, FTE, expected value and realized value, each with its data as-of time (EXE-01, BR-39). Value tiles read "not tracked" until benefits are captured; a tile fed by data older than the workspace freshness threshold is marked stale, and a tile missing some projects' data is marked partial and names them. The executive attention queue lists what needs a person: missing status reports, green-but-risky projects, overdue milestones and gates, pending and approved-but-unapplied change requests, capacity overloads and material budget variance. Each item has an owner, a reason, a severity and a next action, which is to decide, review, rebalance or remind (EXE-02). The wireframe traces the items to their modules: the weekly status of [M12 Journal and Project Reporting](M12-journal-and-project-reporting.md), the green-but-risky flag of [M13 Risk and Dependency](M13-risk-and-dependency.md) and the unapplied CRs of [M11 Change Control, Baseline and Decision Log](M11-change-control-baseline-and-decision-log.md). Portfolio health is shown overall and by dimension, delivery, risk, resource and financial in the MVP and strategic in V2; it is calculated consistently, drills down to the projects behind it and shows which have stale or missing status data (EXE-03, BR-32).

The workspace dashboard is the governance view of one workspace: portfolio health, investment, capacity, risk, governance exceptions and upcoming gates, and a delivery pipeline of the projects per lifecycle stage with the overdue gates and the blocked projects, whose counts equal the projects' current stages (EXE-05). Two requirements are priority P1, which the BRD reads as V2. The executive decision center recommends START, CONTINUE, ACCELERATE, DELAY or STOP for projects, each with its rationale and evidence, and a recommendation stays advisory until an authorized executive approves it (EXE-04, BR-10). The cross-workspace view shows the governance health of each workspace and respects record-level permissions (EXE-06, BR-20).

The module is for the CEO and the executive committee, whose key views are the cockpit and the decision center, and for the PMO, whose key views include the workspace dashboard ([section 6](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#6-stakeholders--personas)). The other four requirements are P0, part of Release 1. The BRD builds M15 to M18 for milestone 1B and expects the executive cockpit to reconcile with project data ([section 18.2](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#182-release-1-delivery-structure-d-01)). Its wireframes are screens 14 (Executive portfolio cockpit) and 15 (Workspace dashboard). The CEO weekly review starts at the cockpit ([section 7.9](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_v4.5.md#79-key-user-journeys)).

The onboarding addendum adds to the attention queue (EXE-02): each open finding of the Portfolio X-ray becomes an item at launch (ONB-13), and, per owner, one item stands for the assumed values awaiting confirmation (ONB-08) and for the overdue delegated tasks (ONB-17) ([addendum, section 13](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_Addendum_Onboarding_v1.1.md#13-amendments-to-brd-v45)). The executive's three framing answers set the grouping of the cockpit and what the home view shows first ([addendum, section 6.3](../requirements/Enterprise_Portfolio_Intelligence_Governance_Platform_BRD_Addendum_Onboarding_v1.1.md#63-the-three-executive-questions)), and the draft cockpit of onboarding (screen 48) is delivered at milestone 1B, with the cockpit.

## What users can do today

Nothing yet. The app is not deployed and has no back end. Its development build runs on mock data and holds one screen of the BRD, the project overview (screen 24, module M10), so no requirement of this module can be used: there is no cockpit, no attention queue, no portfolio health and no workspace dashboard.

The build's navigation does name this module's screens, both phase MVP in the wireframe manifest:

- Executive cockpit, in the Portfolio group of the sidebar, links to `/w/[workspaceId]/cockpit`, the route of screen 14.
- Workspace dashboard, in the same group, links to `/w/[workspaceId]/dashboard`, the route of screen 15.
- The workspace menu of the sidebar lists the workspaces of the mock tenant, and choosing one opens `/w/[workspaceId]/dashboard` for that workspace, screen 15 again.

At narrower widths the two sidebar links sit in the icon rail of a tablet, or in the drawer that the menu button opens, and the workspace menu is in the drawer. Each link opens the shared page for a screen that is not built. It shows, in the English interface, the title of the link (Executive cockpit or Workspace dashboard), the sentence "Not built in the first batch.", the screen number, the path, and the filters that came in the address, or "None". It follows the rule of the record [Final routes, drill filters in the URL, and unbuilt destinations](../../architecture/decisions/routes-and-unbuilt-destinations.md): an MVP screen that is not built already has its final route and a generic page.

The app does not open on a portfolio view: its root address redirects to the project overview of one demo project, the one screen the build has. The decision center (EXE-04) and the cross-workspace view (EXE-06) are V2 requirements, and the navigation has no control for either. The build computes no portfolio figure and no health itself (see [The app never computes a metric or a drill filter](../../architecture/decisions/metrics-are-server-snapshots.md)).

## Where the app differs from the BRD

None yet. Nothing of this module is built, so there is no built behavior to compare with the BRD.

## Open questions

- Do the links that lead from a portfolio view down to a project keep the view's filter, sort and time period, so that the way back returns to the same filtered view? The first review called the drill-downs from the cockpit and from the registry good, and asked that the roadmap, capacity and risk views follow the same deep-link contract, with a "Back to filtered portfolio" action that keeps the filter, sort and time period ([Pre-feasibility review of the BRD, round 1](../../history/reviews/metis-review-tien-kha-thi.md), line 509 of the original). The second review asked to keep the filter and the context sticky between the views that the Product Director walks through, from the registry to the scenario ([Round 2 review](../../history/reviews/metis-review-vong-2.md), line 413 of the original). The third review asked to preserve the filter and the context when going back, and for a filter that persists across views ([Round 3 review](../../history/reviews/metis-review-vong-3.md), lines 951 and 952 of the original). The requirements of this module say that health is drillable to projects (EXE-03) and that an attention item carries a next action (EXE-02), but not what the way back keeps. No decision record settles it. The same question stands with the registry ([M16 Portfolio and Project Registry](M16-portfolio-and-project-registry.md)), the roadmap ([M17 Portfolio Roadmap](M17-portfolio-roadmap.md)), the capacity view ([M18 Resource and Capacity](M18-resource-and-capacity.md)) and the risk and dependency view ([M13 Risk and Dependency](M13-risk-and-dependency.md)).
